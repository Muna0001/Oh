Oh!

Two music tools from one person who makes records with them: a polysynth inspired by the classic sounds of the 80s that runs in your browser, and a sample organizer for the library you already have.

Oh a Synth

Six voices, inspired by the 80s.

Exact DCO tuning, a self-oscillating ladder filter, one honest ADSR, and the chorus that made the original famous. The whole signal path runs per-sample in your browser — no download, no signup.

Play it in your browser

Oh a Comber

Every sample you own, findable.

Oh a Comber scans your sample folders where they sit and turns them into a searchable, filterable library. Preview with waveforms, then drag the real file straight onto a track. It never moves or renames anything.
