Oh a Chord

Out now on the App Store

Press one key. Get a full chord.

A one-octave iPad keyboard. Press one key and the whole chord plays. Chord Type and Modifier buttons set its colour. Key Mode keeps every press inside the key you pick. A held chord becomes a strum, a harp sweep, or an arpeggio. No music theory required.
